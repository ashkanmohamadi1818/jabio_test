Privacy

What Jabio does and does not do with your information.

Draft: written in plain language to match what this site actually does. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer.

The short version

Analysis runs in your browser. If you create an account, we store your email, a password hash and analyses you choose to save. Session and security cookies keep you signed in.

What is stored on your device

  • Your choice of language and light or dark theme, so the site opens the way you left it.
  • Without signing in, up to three saved analyses stay in this browser. Clearing site data removes them. Signed-in saves are stored in your account; browser saves are copied only if you choose to copy them.
  • If you close the small notice about anonymous counts (only shown when counting is switched on), we remember that you closed it.

The map and other services

  • The map on the analysis page loads its background tiles from OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org). Like any website, that service can see your IP address and which part of the map you are looking at. We do not control that service; see its own policy.
  • The “See the place” links (Google Street View and Mapillary) open those sites in a new tab and put the coordinates of the spot in the link. That happens only if you click, and from then on their own policies apply. 3D buildings come from the same map tiles as the map.
  • Fonts, scripts and all place and population data are served from this site itself.
  • The “Add a missing place” button opens openstreetmap.org in a new tab. What you do there is between you and OpenStreetMap.

Anonymous visit counts (optional)

  • This is off unless the site owner switches it on, and then a notice appears. When on, the site sends a short message for each page view or analysis: the event name, the page, the language, the theme and, for analyses, the city and the type of business. It never sends map coordinates, addresses or anything you type. This counting does not use a tracking cookie.
  • If your browser sends “Do Not Track” or Global Privacy Control, nothing is counted.

Contact form

The contact form does not send anything to a server. It opens your own email app with the message filled in; you decide whether to send it.

Your choices

You can remove individual account analyses in your shortlist and clear browser data in your browser settings. Contact the site operator for account deletion or access requests.

Changes

If this changes, this page changes first and the date below is updated.
