Questions, mistakes, ideas, or data to share.

What helps most

  • A wrong or missing shop on the map: fix it in OpenStreetMap (the “Add a missing place” button), then tell us if it still looks wrong.
  • A result that looks wrong: send the city, the business, the radius and the spot, and what you expected there.
  • Pedestrian counts, census figures or a data licence you can share: these are the things that would improve Jabio most.

Common answers are on the FAQ.
