## Streets next to a transit stop against streets far from one

Streets within 120 m of a transit stop (9824): mean index **23**. Streets more than 400 m from any stop (5442): mean index **4**. A model that works should put people at stops; the stops are origins and destinations in the model, so this is a sanity check, not independent evidence.

## Disconnected fragments

The street graph has 222 components; 3.3% of its nodes sit in fragments of under 300 nodes (streets that are not connected to the rest in OpenStreetMap, so people cannot reach them in the model). Of the 717 streets inside the area with index 90 or more, 0 are in such fragments.

## What is missing

- Pedestrian counts to calibrate: until then these are relative indices only.
- Weights of attractions, transit stops and workers are judgement (table ATTRACT in the script).
- Population comes from the WorldPop model; its error is large in places (see DATA-EXTRACTION.md).
- Pavement quality, crossings, steps, slope, weather, time of day, and who is walking are not modelled.
- Edge effects: walkers who start more than 1.5 km outside the area are not included.
