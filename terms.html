Terms of use

The ground rules for using Jabio.

Draft: written in plain language to match what this site actually does. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer.

What this is

Jabio offers free tools and open data. Jabio scores places for a business using open data. A score is a shortlist to go and look at, not advice or a prediction of sales.

Use at your own judgement

  • Results can be wrong or incomplete. Places missing from both OpenStreetMap and Overture Maps are not counted, and Overture can still list a shop that has closed. Population is a model (WorldPop 2020), not a census, and can be far off in some districts. Rent, income and real foot traffic are not included. Visit the place and check the facts before signing a lease or spending money.
  • The service is provided as it is, without any promise of accuracy or availability. To the extent the law allows, we are not responsible for decisions you make using it.

Data and licences

Place data is © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), completed with Overture Maps places (CDLA-Permissive-2.0, Apache-2.0, CC0). Population is WorldPop 2020 (CC BY 4.0), in Tehran adjusted with building footprints from OpenStreetMap and Microsoft ML Buildings via Overture Maps (ODbL). Other datasets list their own licences on the Data page. If you reuse them, keep the credit and follow the licence.
