Location field guide

From a promising score to an informed decision.

Compare locationsYour shortlistWorked examplesLocation field guide

Checklist progress stays in this browser and applies to your current field visit. Print a copy for each candidate.

01

Define your customer

Write down the offer, price range, opening hours and who would come. Choose the business type and customer radius in Jabio before comparing streets.

02

Build a shortlist

Compare two spots in the same city with identical settings. Look at demand, access, competitors and plausible ranges together. Overlapping ranges do not establish a reliable winner.

03

Count on the street

Visit on a weekday and a weekend, at opening, peak and quiet times. For each fixed 15-minute interval record passers-by, likely customers and entries to nearby shops. Repeat counts; a busy hour is not a daily average.

04

Check the unit

Check visibility, walking access, loading, parking, utilities, ventilation and permission for signage. Ask the relevant local authority about the exact licence and permitted use for this address before signing.

05

Stress-test the numbers

Use the report’s break-even calculator with your own rent, wages, average sale and gross margin. Separate one-time fit-out costs and deposit from monthly costs. Try lower sales and higher costs; leave room for working capital.

06

Record the decision

Keep the report, observations, quotes and unanswered questions together. Resolve the gaps with field evidence. A high score is a reason to investigate a place; it does not predict profit.
