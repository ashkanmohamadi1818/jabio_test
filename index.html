How it works

Three steps, no guessing

Choose

Pick the business, the city and how far your customers come from. Add who they are if you know.

Count

Jabio counts what is inside that radius on a 250 m grid: buildings, offices, schools, shops, roads, bus stops and your rivals.

Decide

You get ranked areas, the reasons, the risks, and a report. Then you go and look at the street yourself.

The score

One formula, in the open

No hidden model. Four ingredients, weights that are judgement not a fit, income level for concepts that depend on it, and an honest list of what is missing.

What goes into a score

Room beside rivals
Your rivals against the number this much demand normally carries. Nearby things count more than far ones.

What Jabio does not know

  • Rent, purchase prices and income. Check them before you decide.
  • Real foot traffic. Streets and bus stops are only a stand-in.
  • Residents. They are a WorldPop 2020 model spread over mapped buildings, not a census.
  • Shops nobody has mapped yet. OpenStreetMap is thinner in some cities, and every result says so.
